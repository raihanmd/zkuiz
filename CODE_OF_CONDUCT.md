# Code of conduct

Treat other participants with respect. Discuss the work and explain disagreements without personal attacks. Welcome questions and constructive criticism.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and sharing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to address disruptive behavior. They will consider the context and severity of each incident.

Use GitHub's report-content controls to flag abusive content. You can also [report abuse privately to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For security vulnerabilities in the project, follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).
